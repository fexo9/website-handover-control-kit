# Website Handover Control Kit

**You paid for your website — make sure you own it.**

A complete kit for small business owners who hire web designers, freelancers, or agencies: 12 pre-hire ownership clauses, a 32-point handover checklist, a credential vault template, and a step-by-step recovery playbook — so your domain, hosting, and admin access end up in *your* hands, not your designer's.

## What's inside the paid kit ($19, Commercial license)

- **12 pre-hire ownership clauses** — plain-language contract language covering domain, hosting, CMS access, email/DNS, analytics, licenses, IP assignment, and a no-hostage clause for payment disputes. Includes a copy-paste email to send your designer.
- **32-point handover checklist** — verify every item yourself before paying the final invoice.
- **Credential vault template** — one page listing every account your site depends on.
- **6-phase take-back recovery playbook** — for when control was already lost: evidence first, 2 formal letter templates, the registrar dispute route, and exactly when to involve a lawyer. Includes a recovery tracker.
- **20-point post-handover audit** — with an auto-scoring spreadsheet.
- **Fictional end-to-end worked example** — a bakery owner recovering her domain in 6 weeks.
- **7 FAQs + honest limits** — what the kit can and cannot do, stated plainly.
- **In 11 languages** — English (full) + Arabic, Mandarin, French, Spanish, Russian, German, Japanese, Portuguese, Hindi, Italian (core documents).
