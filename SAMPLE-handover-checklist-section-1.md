## 1. Domain (the most important section — do this first)

- [ ] The domain is in a registrar account **you** can log into.
      How to verify: log in at the registrar's site yourself. Do not accept a screenshare.
- [ ] **You** are named as the registrant, with **your** email as the registrant contact.
      How to verify: check the domain's contact details inside the registrar account (or via ICANN Lookup). The name and email must be yours.
- [ ] Domain privacy is set the way **you** chose (on or off), controlled from your account.
- [ ] Auto-renew is ON, charged to **your** card, and the expiry date is more than 60 days away.
      How to verify: open the billing/renewal page and read the card's last four digits and the expiry date.
- [ ] You have the transfer authorization code (Auth/EPP code) or know exactly where to get it in under 5 minutes.
- [ ] The domain is not within a transfer lock you didn't agree to (check transfer status in the registrar panel).
